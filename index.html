Selected work

Clients are anonymised. Each case covers the problem, what I did and what moved. Filter by the kind of work you care about.

Global consulting business, cloud Building the GTM control tower for a hyper-scaling practice RFP turnaround from about 2 months to 4 days
Problem
Pre-sales and delivery were scaling faster than the operating model under them. Leaders couldn't see the pipeline clearly and responses were slow.
What I did
As chief of staff to the strategy director, I built offer structures and centralised pricing access. I also redesigned delivery motions and built deal visibility tools.
Result
  • RFP turnaround cut from about 2 months to 4 days
  • Dashboard daily users up 9x
  • Rolled out across 8 markets
Global enterprise technology Redesigning a partner incentives estate before an ERP move 68% fewer incentive plans
Problem
Dozens of incentive plans, unclear partner classifications and a legacy structure that couldn't support the coming ERP migration.
What I did
Assessed channel performance, partner types and product hierarchies. Then rebuilt the incentives framework to cut plans and tie payouts to revenue.
Result
  • Incentive plan count down 68%
  • Payouts better aligned to revenue, so channel spend became easier to forecast
  • A structure the new ERP can run without manual workarounds
Retail and mobility Standardising payments across a 480-site network 8 scenarios, one target state, 3,500+ devices
Problem
Fragmented tools and inconsistent payment flows in a $4.3B revenue business. All of it had to move to one platform.
What I did
Mapped every scenario permutation, defined process ownership, documented target-state risks and dependencies, and planned the product transition.
Result
  • 8 engagement scenarios standardised
  • Transition logic for 3,500+ devices across 480 sites
Multi-business enterprise Lifting quote-to-cash maturity across five businesses Value realisation pulled from year 3 to year 1
Problem
Five business units sat at different maturity levels, with friction across quoting, commercial process and services operations. $1.2B turnover was at stake.
What I did
Ran current and target-state workshops and found the bottlenecks. Designed a staged path that reuses existing tools first and builds only the gaps.
Result
  • A phased roadmap across 5 business units
  • First value expected in year 1 instead of year 3
Sales and marketing shared services Designing what a global services centre should own 40+ capabilities mapped, 187 roles structured
Problem
Nobody had a clear view of what to centralise or who owned each process. How the centre and business units should work together was also unclear.
What I did
Assessed the current state and mapped 40+ capability areas. Designed target-state processes and matched the organisation design to hiring waves.
Result
  • 47 detailed process areas designed
  • Future structure agreed for 187 FTE
Technology hardware, in-house Running commercial growth, pricing and incentives for a country business Quota met in 5 of 5 years, up from 2 of 5
Problem
Commercial teams needed sharper targeting, firmer pricing discipline and cleaner incentives to grow complex accounts.
What I did
Designed quota logic and revised compensation levers. Improved category mix and used customer-level analysis to grow large accounts.
Result
  • Above 100% of target across multiple years
  • Premium portfolio and attach sales up 200%
